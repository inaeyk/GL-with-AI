## Rigorous nonuniversality and robustness distinction

Keeping all lower-order data and the complete source fixed, add a compact homogeneous returning CPM pair on the slice. It is a legitimate axial Gauss-completed perturbation, with zero first-order changes of the spherical charges. Choose its support in an open portion with $u_\Sigma>U$. It then leaves every cut through $U$ unchanged. At a simple oscillatory pole the graph pairing of these real data has full complex rank. Otherwise both $w|_\Sigma$ and $D_\Sigma w|_\Sigma$ would have one common complex phase throughout the support interval, by independently testing displacement and normal velocity. The tangential derivative of $w$ would also have that phase. Since $|T'|<1$, taking the difference of these derivatives gives $w_t=-s_qw$ with the same phase, impossible where $w\ne0$ and $\Im s_q\ne0$. The residue can therefore be shifted or canceled at will by permitted changes of upper data. The explicit fixed-canonical preparation removes this freedom by definition.

An arbitrary smooth compact *auxiliary* forcing at later retarded times can likewise vary the continued coefficient without changing the earlier cuts. This is a counterexample to inference from a finite trace alone. It is not a modification of the fixed vacuum hierarchy. Two different tapers of the same source differ by their bilinear pairing with $(\chi_{c,1}-\chi_{c,2})S$, which might vanish for a particular source or band. Separately, a smooth compact test forcing $LZ$ supported strictly above the slice has zero pole numerator by integration against the adjoint solution; the causal solution $Z$ has zero outgoing trace. Nonzero local forcing by itself is insufficient.

The finite-cut waveform map is continuous in a sufficiently high finite smooth data norm by the strip estimates. Its nonzero response thus persists on an open set of nearby finite-data preparations. For a defined simple residue with continuous source and data pairing, a nonzero continuous linear functional of the axial seed would also imply generic nonvanishing outside its closed proper kernel. The [signed enclosure and continuity argument](SIGNED-RESIDUE.md) establish this genericity on the stated fixed-support, fixed-canonical linear space and an open neighbourhood within the normalized preparation class. The upper-data nonuniversality result applies when the independent upper prescription is allowed to vary.
