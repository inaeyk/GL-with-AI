## Launch graph and distribution

Write \(Q=Q_{2z}\). In the background tortoise chart \(A_{2x}=fA_{2r}\), so the constrained equations imply
\[
\lambda r^2(A_{2x}+T'A_{2t})
 =Q_t+T'Q_x-\mathcal D^r+T'f\mathcal D^t .
\]
The tangent on the graph is \(\partial_x+T'\partial_t\). The left side vanishes for the fixed tangential canonical pair. Also \(Q|_\Sigma=0\) by the fixed electric density, since \(\pi^r=(PF)^{\tau r}=(PF)^{tr}=Q_z\) in this chart. Hence the slice flux for \(\Psi_2=2Q/(\lambda r)\) is exactly \(-2\phi(p-T'q)/\lambda\) in \(dx\). For \(J(x)=\int_{T(x)}^\infty\phi q\,dt\), direct differentiation gives
\[
\int_{T(x)}^\infty[-\phi p_t-\phi q_x]dt
=\phi(p-T'q)|_\Sigma+\int_{T(x)}^\infty(\phi_t p+\phi_x q)\,dt-J'(x).
\]
The launch term cancels. On a radial interval \([a,b]\) the remaining boundary is \(+J(a)-J(b)\). Converting \(dx=dr/f\) gives the stated \(a^\dagger_a\) coefficients. The homogeneous scalar equation for \(w=\phi/r\) gives \(\phi_{tt}-\phi_{xx}+2f\phi_x/r=-\lambda f\phi/r^2\), hence \(d a^\dagger_{tr}=-\phi/r^4\) as used in the bulk.

The distribution \(d\Theta\wedge A_j=\delta(\tau)d\tau\wedge A_j\) vanishes because the tangential pullback vanishes for \(j=1,2\). In adapted coordinates \(\sqrt{|b|}=N\sqrt{\gamma}\), \(n=-N d\tau\), and antisymmetry give \(\pi^i=(PF)^{\tau i}\). The step derivative generated by \(E_0[\Theta A_j]\) is therefore \(\delta(\tau)(P_0F_j)^{\tau a}=-\delta(\tau)\sum_{m=1}^j(P_mF_{j-m})^{\tau a}\). Its time component vanishes identically. This gives the stated distributional identity, including the compact polarization and divergence equation. At baseline there is nonzero tangential data, so its graph terms and the curl reconstruction commutator remain. These cancellations use the exact curl and the fixed canonical data in the same completed chart. They should not be imported into a scalar source taper without its own cutoff derivatives and boundary bookkeeping.

## Continued horizon and local holomorphy

Since \(v=t+x\), the unit-Jacobian transformation in \((t,r)\) gives \(q=fr^{-2}\mathcal D^v-p\), and \(\mathcal C^{tr}=\mathcal C^{vr}\). In the EF chart
\[
-p_t-q_x=-f\left[r^{-2}\partial_v\mathcal D^v+
\partial_r(fr^{-2}\mathcal D^v-p)\right].
\]
Together with the \(f\mathcal C\) term this proves the strong EF source factor \(f\). In the continued transform \(\widehat p=e^{sx}p_H(s,r)\), \(\widehat q=-\widehat p+O(fe^{sx})\), and \((rh)'=(s+O(f))rh\) on the ingoing branch. The separated bulk then permits the leading term \(-2sr_0p_H(s,r_0)e^{2sx}\). Its integral diverges for \(\Re s<0\) if the coefficient is nonzero. The actual coefficient need not be assumed nonzero to require the robust finite split. In the constant horizon model with \(q=-p\) and the radial factors exactly \(e^{sx}\), the strong transformed derivative is zero. On \([a,b]\) the bulk is \(-p_H(e^{2sb}-e^{2sa})\), canceled exactly by the two radial boundary terms. The positive sign of the finite left boundary in PAIRING (3) is therefore correct. Choosing \(x_a\) below the compact launch support leaves no graph datum in the left piece.

The certified noncoincidence of both shifted pencils, together with the previous uniform complex-ray and real-endpoint bounds, suffices for local holomorphy of the formal ordered radial numerator at the exact simple pole. This conclusion uses the same continued Jost branches as the certificate, compact graph distributions, the separated and horizon-regular completed EF coefficients, and the full two-polarization inhomogeneous ingoing prescription. Equivalently the regular EF Frobenius solution has no resonant denominator \(n(n+2r_0s_1)\) there.
