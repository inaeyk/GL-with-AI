## External papers

- S. C. Collingbourne, *The Gregory–Laflamme Instability of the Schwarzschild Black String Exterior*. Inspected theorem statement and version metadata. Theorem 1.1 and §§3.2, 3.4, 4.1 give the growing exterior mode and regular construction. The numerical rate and metric reconstruction used here come from the audited repository. [arXiv:2007.08441v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.08441v1)
- A. Ishibashi and H. Kodama, *Perturbations and Stability of Static Black Holes in Higher Dimensions*, §5.1, (5.2)–(5.3). General warped-product vector perturbation equations provide a cross-check on the local axial reduction. [arXiv:1103.6148v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.6148v2)
- H. Kodama, A. Ishibashi and O. Seto, *Brane World Cosmology*. Specialist A inspected the general warped vector equations (24)–(36). [hep-th/0004160v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0004160v3)
- K. Martel and E. Poisson, *Gravitational perturbations of the Schwarzschild spacetime*. Inspected §V, including (5.13)–(5.20), and §VI, including (6.11)–(6.16), for axial invariant, shear and flux. [arXiv:gr-qc/0502028v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0502028v1)
- E. W. Leaver, *An analytic representation for the quasi-normal modes of Kerr black holes*, Proc. R. Soc. A **402**, 285 (1985), Table 1. The Schwarzschild axial quadrupole frequency is used as an empirical spectral benchmark. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1985.0119)
- E. W. Leaver, *Spectral decomposition of the perturbation response of the Schwarzschild geometry*, Phys. Rev. D **34**, 384 (1986), §§II–III and (89)–(93), with erratum **38**, 725 (1988). Pole, cut and prompt contributions and extended-source continuation provide the spectral context. [Published paper and erratum link](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.34.384)
- A. Bachelot and A. Motet-Bachelot, *Les résonances d’un trou noir de Schwarzschild*, Ann. IHP Phys. Théor. **59**, 3 (1993). Inspected publication abstract on meromorphic continuation of the RW scattering matrix. [Archive](https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPA_1993__59_1_3_0/)
- M. Casals and A. C. Ottewill, *Analytic Investigation of the Branch Cut of the Green Function in Schwarzschild Spacetime*, Phys. Rev. D **87**, 064010 (2013). Specialist C inspected §II, (2.1)–(2.8), for the outgoing branches and cut, with its separate Green sign convention. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.87.064010)
