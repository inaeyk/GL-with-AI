# Negative and limited results

- A mean-zero \(R^{-5}\) compact coefficient can couple adjacent harmonics with an \(O(k_m)\) same-order forcing pairing. The small radial weight at a fixed shell does not control arbitrarily large compact harmonics. This is a manufactured first-variation test, not an evolving vacuum solution. - A near-identity single similarity \(T_\lambda=1+O(\lambda)\) leaves the \(O(\lambda)\) variation of an operator \(I+\lambda A\) unchanged. A near-identity positive symmetrizer \(M_\lambda\) likewise leaves the first-order skew part \(A-A^*\) in \(M_\lambda(I+\lambda A)-(I+\lambda A)^*M_\lambda\). Separate paired multipliers are necessary in the displayed correction. A mass-only diagonalization can instead alter the mixed radial kinetic term even at zero amplitude.
- Uniform clock convergence alone is insufficient for strong \(L^2\). Take \(T_\epsilon=u+\epsilon^2\sin(u/\epsilon^2)/2\), \(T'\in[1/2,3/2]\), and a unit constant comparison news on padded cuts. The clock-normalized error divided by \(\epsilon^2\) has squared norm \(\int (1-T')^2/T'\,du\) on the pulled-back window. Its lower limit is at least \(|I|/12\). The weak pairing still converges.
- Uniform field control alone does not ensure an \(H^1\) radiation field. In the enlarged scalar equation, take \(a=B=C=h=0\) and use \(A=\partial_v\) of a smoothly cap-dependent regularization of \(\chi(u)|u-u_0|^{1/4}\), cut off near the graph. The primitive converges uniformly to a continuous function with non-square-integrable derivative.
- The pure mean compact correction does not symmetrize the full nonmean system, and the first-order nonmean correction does not bound the quadratic remainder in its coefficients. Supplementary integration functions, exact first-jet exterior fluxes and common-clock geometric trace matching are unevaluated. The exact wave and nonlinear energy conclusions remain conditional.
