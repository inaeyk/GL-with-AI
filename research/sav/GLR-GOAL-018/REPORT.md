## Witness construction and ringdown interpretation

For \(\theta=\pi u/d\), the rejection test is the normalized real function
\[
 g_R=(\sin\theta-\sin(13\theta)/13)(1+\tfrac12\cos\theta)Q_{\rm mid}/\|\cdot\|_2 .
\]
Both endpoint values and first derivatives vanish. For a unit test, a directed upper bound on \(\langle g_R,N_{21}\rangle\) below \(\inf_P(\langle g_R,P\rangle-\|P\|_2/2)\) proves strict rejection. The expression is concave in the complex residue coordinates at fixed exponents, so the minimum over each box is attained at a corner. Varying the exponents moves the expression by at most \(3/2\) times the induced \(L^2\) template error. The directed lower bounds are \(0.17469309776\) for \(Q\) and \(0.37308701510\) for \(Q+S_*\). The first exceeds the earlier sine-test threshold with endpoint compatibility; the second is less permissive than the earlier scalar-only sine-test threshold. The separate \(g_M\) is the normalized \(\sin^2\theta\cos\theta\). For the nuisance distance, project onto the orthonormalized \(\sin^2\theta\{1,\cos\theta,\cos2\theta+2/3\}\). If \(a,b,c\) denote the projected physical baseline, clock tangent and archived return, the directed cross product gives \((a\times b)\cdot c/\|a\times b\|>0.05238\). The projected \(b_j=-\langle g_j',N_{01}\rangle\) uses integration by parts and the certified baseline error, without using a numerical derivative of the baseline.

The strong archived rejection ceilings and the positive matched-change threshold remain distinct. An error allowance \(0.31731\) or \(0.80484\) could contain a vanishing return since the archive norm is only about \(0.08741\). For a physical norm lower bound the strong target is \(E_{21}<0.08741378900667\). Fixed-residue comparison for the return says nothing by itself about an unrestricted damped sinusoid fit. Individual spectral allocations still require bounds on the causal response beyond pole terms.
