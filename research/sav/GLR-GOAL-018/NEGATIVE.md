# Failure modes and counterexamples

1. **Weak-to-strong inference fails even at infinity.** Take a smooth \(Q(v)\) zero near the graph and one near the cap and thereafter, \(\chi\in C^\infty_c(0,d)\), \(h_n=n^{-1/2}\chi\sin(nu)\). Then \(w_n=h_nQ\), \(F_n=4h_nQ'\), \(G_n=0\), \(H_n=-Vh_nQ\) solve the scalar return exactly with zero graph/front jets. The undifferentiated weak norms are \(O(n^{-1/2})\) and news has norm asymptotic to \(\sqrt{n/2}\|\chi\|_2\). Subtracting fixed smooth compact profiles with coefficients \(O(n^{-1/2})\) kills any finite set of \(H^1\) witness moments exactly (invert on the range of their finite-rank moment map). This is a manufactured estimator control. It does not alter the physical source.
2. **Early cap endpoint remains necessary.** A cap shear rising smoothly from zero to one on \([-\varepsilon,0]\) and constant on \(I\) has zero late cap news and \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) early cap shear norm. Its endpoint value is one. With no outer forcing the Volterra evolution keeps the shear at least \(1/2\) on \(I\), since \(e^{DJ_c}-1<1/2\) at the fixed cap. The outgoing news receives the strictly negative integral \(-\int Vw/4\,dv\) and its \(L^2\) norm is bounded away from zero as \(\varepsilon\to0\).
3. **Volume residual alone misses the graph second jet.** For a smooth \(A(u)\) supported within \(I\), let \(e_\varepsilon=A(u)(v-b(u))^2\psi((v-b(u))/\varepsilon)\) with \(\psi\) smooth, compactly supported and \(\psi(0)\ne0\). It has exact zero first graph jets and a vanishing finite-\(p\) volume residual, while \(e_{uu}|_\Sigma=2(b')^2 A\psi(0)\) remains fixed. The commuted error must include the graph trace or a cancellation that fixes it.
4. **The old sine tapers have nonflat jets.** Enforcing only \(g(d)=0\) leaves the terminal row and, for a zero extension at the left, the \(u=0\) seam. The corrected adjoint and flat-jet witnesses address this.
5. **The physical numerical gate is open.** Directed arithmetic on a sampled candidate, a small grid difference and the inherited exterior bound do not enclose the full compact physical error. A trial without a conforming mixed residual does not give a continuum estimate. The audit’s malformed-archive failure is addressed by requiring the pinned digest before moment arithmetic.
