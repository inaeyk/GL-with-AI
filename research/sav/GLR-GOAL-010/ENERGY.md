# Signed Einstein budget and asymptotic normalization

Let \(\mathcal E=G^{(1)}\) and \(\nabla_a\mathfrak W^a(p,q)=p:\mathcal E[q]-q:\mathcal E[p]\). With a lower-index metric variation, the Einstein–Hilbert bulk variation is \(-G:p/(16\pi G_5)\). For \(\omega_{\rm EH}(p,q)=\delta_p\theta(q)-\delta_q\theta(p)\), use \(\mathfrak W=-16\pi G_5\omega_{\rm EH}\) with a fixed improvement and corner convention. Write \(\mathsf T=\partial_t=\partial_\tau=\partial_v\). The quadratic energy coefficient of the real \(n=\pm2\) field is generated by
\[
 j^a=-\frac{\mathfrak W^a(q,\mathcal L_{\mathsf T}q)}{32\pi G_5},\qquad
 \nabla_a j^a=\frac{q:\mathcal L_{\mathsf T}Q_E-Q_E:\mathcal L_{\mathsf T}q}{32\pi G_5}.
\]
The \(1/2\) relative to the symplectic current gives the physical quadratic coefficient. The outgoing flat TT check yields \(j^r=-\langle \partial_t q:\partial_r q\rangle/(32\pi G_5)\). In \((\tau,r)\) integrate \(\sqrt{-\bar g}\,j^\tau\) on slices, \(+\sqrt{-\bar g}\,j^r\) at the outer tube and \(-\sqrt{-\bar g}\,j^r\) at the horizon. The result is
\[
 \Delta E_{\rm stored}+E_{\rm tube}+E_{\rm horizon}=W_{\rm source}.
\]
Extra domain sides add their oriented flux. These forced finite-domain terms are signed. An alternative current subtracts \(\mathsf T^a(q:Q_E)/(32\pi G_5)\), giving volume work \(-Q_E:\mathcal L_{\mathsf T}q/(16\pi G_5)\) and shifting slice storage while keeping fixed-radius boundary fluxes. Fix one representative before comparing. Sourced gauge changes can contribute to volume and boundary terms. The representative above vanishes pointwise on the pure separated response, since \(\mathcal L_{\mathsf T}q=sq\) and \(\mathfrak W\) is antisymmetric.

[Hollands–Wald, v4, Eqs. (16)–(24), (44), (105)–(106)](https://arxiv.org/html/1201.0463v4) gives the symplectic conventions and boundary qualifications. Positivity results for homogeneous perturbations with prescribed boundary gauge do not establish positivity of this forced finite-tube budget.

## Positive scalar estimate

The inherited positive supersolution \(w=e^{-r/2}r/D\) at \(a=4\pi^2/25>3/2\) gives \(H_K=D_x^\dagger D_x+\mathcal U\), \(D_x=\partial_x|_t+S_d,\ S_d=-\partial_x\log w,\ \mathcal U=V_K+S_d'-S_d^2>0\) for \(r>1\). The physical potential satisfies \(V_K/f\to-1.09450347539\) at the horizon. For the master,
\[
 e=\tfrac12(|\psi_t|^2+|D_x\psi|^2+\mathcal U|\psi|^2),
 \quad j_s=-\Re(\overline{\psi_t}D_x\psi),\quad
 \partial_t e+\partial_x|_t j_s=\Re(\overline{\psi_t}\mathfrak F).
\]
The \(\tau\)-slice density is \(e+bj_s\) and \(E_\tau=\int(e+bj_s)\,dx\ge\tfrac12\int(2-f)|\psi_\tau|^2\,dr\). At the regular horizon \(j_s=-|\psi_v|^2\). With decay or a causal exhaustion at infinity and \(\mathfrak F=-fr\mathscr J/D\), the scalar storage plus accumulated horizon flux \(D_\tau\) obeys
\[
 \sqrt{D_\tau}\le\sqrt{E_0}
   +2^{-1/2}\int_0^\tau\left\|\frac{r\mathscr J}{D\sqrt{2-f}}\right\|_{L^2(dr)}d\tau' .
\]
This estimate controls the scalar master with radial degeneracy at the horizon. It is a distinct identity from the signed Einstein budget. [Positive form proof](../physics/goal003_master_independent.md)

## Outgoing wave-zone flux

For positive frequency and outgoing momentum \(p_\omega=\sqrt{\omega^2-K^2}\), the \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) TT monopole polarization of unit norm is \(e_0=(e_\theta e_\theta+e_\varphi e_\varphi-2e_L e_L)/\sqrt6\) with \(e_L=(-p_\omega,\omega,0,0)/K\) as an asymptotic covector. Raw equal angular strain amplitude \(A_{\rm ang}\) gives norm \(6|A_{\rm ang}|^2\). A complex monochromatic \(+K\) coefficient \(P/r\) plus conjugate gives \(P_{\rm out}/\epsilon^4=\omega p_\omega(P:\overline P)/(4G_4)\). The factor of two is from the exact compact average. For the derivative master \(\psi=rU/D\), \(C_\sigma=2i\omega A_{\rm ang,\sigma}\) at positive frequency. With inverse Fourier measure \(d\omega/(2\pi)\) and \(\sigma=\pm\) labeling both positive-frequency compact channels,
\[
 \frac{E_{\rm out}}{\epsilon^4}
  =\frac{3}{16\pi G_4}\sum_{\sigma=\pm}
    \int_K^\infty\frac{p_\omega}{\omega}|C_\sigma(\omega)|^2\,d\omega .
\]
This assumes a controlled outgoing packet projection and wave-zone limit. The non-derivative affine master instead has \(A_{\rm ang}=-C_Y/2\), so its corresponding flux density uses \(\omega p_\omega |C_Y|^2\) with the same packet prefactor. Keep the two master conventions distinct. No numerical outgoing coefficient, positive lower bound for Z, or partition between horizon and escape was obtained.
