# GL Radiation research roadmap — long horizon, independent from production

## Scientific targets

**R1: exact continuum channels (now).**
Complete 5D SO(3) reduction, GL physical master mode, n=0 radion news,
massive KK singlet classification and mass/tension normalization.
Outcome: audited identities and selection rules.

**R2: second-order early radiation (first extended Codex Goal).**
Derive constrained n=0 quadratic source of n=+/-1 unstable mode,
retarded Green-function transfer, weakly nonlinear energy and spectrum,
possible exact cancellations. Output: C0(k) or rigorous obstruction,
with gauge/normalization and errors.

**R3: weakly nonlinear bulge model.**
Develop amplitude dynamics, timescale shift with epsilon, first-bulge
rollover model and source matching; assess where the expansion fails at
A=O(1). Output: conditional predictions linked to invariant geometric
formation markers, not manufactured percentage claims.

**R4: massive-channel and global budget.**
Construct canonical n!=0 gravitational symplectic flux, dispersive
propagation, KK mode-by-mode waveforms and near-zone/Horizon energy
balance. Output: a validated massive-channel extraction contract.

**R5: late cascade and total energy.**
Conditional scaling/bounds from local radii, string segment energy,
generation multiplicity, high-frequency tail and final charge changes.
Output: test of early-dominance conjecture with explicit assumptions.
Do not demand full pinch-off to establish an early energy measurement.

**R6: engineering collaboration, following Michael's readiness gates.**
Develop observational data interfaces, independent manufactured-wave
acceptance tests, AMR/refinement error budgets, physical boundary
conditions, and extraction validity. Actual implementation and
production runs are Michael-led and need explicit authorization.

## Distinct evidence/readiness gates

- R1/R2 theoretical derivations can proceed with no qualified GL
  numerical solution.
- R6 physical extraction cannot claim acceptance until the existing
  M2-C GP control/physical GL mode hurdle is cleared or an independently
  approved alternative evolution passes equivalent gates.
- Literature conjectures, symbolic checks, code fixtures and production
  numerical evidence must remain separate.
- Questions and negative results are scientific deliverables, not
  nuisances to silently bypass.

## Collaboration cadence

As each meaningful analytical result matures, give Michael a small
read-only handoff with field conventions, needed numerical quantities,
expected scaling, false-positive tests, and source/commit provenance.
Pau receives a focused scientific question when his expertise can
resolve a specific ambiguity. No automatic branch merging or simulation.
