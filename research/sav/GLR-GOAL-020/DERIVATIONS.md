The axial jet changes neither spherical ADM charge nor the circumference at the ordered levels considered here. The admissible tangent space in this chosen class is the image of \(\mathcal H\) by this graph rule, with all its induced jets. ## Absolute continuum bounds

Write \(U=\|h\|_{\mathcal H}\). First take smooth compact profiles. For \(j\le3\), the fundamental theorem of calculus from the left support endpoint gives \(\|h^{(j)}\|_\infty\le \sqrt\delta U\le U\). The graph Killing energy of the baseline is
\[
 E_{0,\Sigma}=\int_a^d (|h'|^2+V|h|^2/[2(1-p)])\,du\le 4U^2 ,
\]
where \(p=-1/r\) and \(V=f(6/r^2-3/r^3)\). The domain has \(r>3/2\) since \(u_\Sigma(3/2)=-3/2+2\log 2>d\), and it lies in the wider GOAL-013 strip ending at \(8.407362\). Thus \(M_0\le2U\). For scalar pointwise news, \(w_{0,v}|_\Sigma=0\) and \(w_{0,u}|_\Sigma=h'\). Integrating the scalar wave equation twice yields \(\|w_0\|_\infty\le e^{(9/4)\delta}\|h\|_\infty\). Since \(\int_b^\infty V\,dv/4\le9/4\), \(\|N_{01}\|_\infty\le\sqrt\delta[1+(9/4)e^{(9/4)\delta}]U<2U\).

Use the positive commuted estimates in GOAL-013 B-SOURCE §§2–3, replacing its \(A_*\) there by \(U\) in the graph algebra. For example \(w_{0,tt}|_\Sigma=h''-Vh/(1-p^2)\). ## Explicit ball, temporal bracket and nonlinear bridge

The canonical profile is \(h_*=\sqrt\delta\,c_b e^{-t_b}\), \(t_b=[y(1-y)]^{-1}\) for \(y=(u-a)/\delta\). The normalization \(\|h_*'\|_2=1\) gives \(c_b\le e^4/\sqrt2<40\). Since \(t_b=1/y+1/(1-y)\), \(|t_b^{(j)}|\le j!t_b^{j+1}\). For \(j\le4\) this gives \(|(e^{-t_b})^{(j)}|\le10t_b^{2j}e^{-t_b}\). With \(\sup_{t\ge4}t^n e^{-t}<10^4\) for \(0\le n\le8\) and \(\delta^{-7/2}<2000\), \(\|h_*\|_{\mathcal H}<2\cdot10^{10}\). Use the looser \(B=10^{11}\) for the whole ball below. For \(e=h-h_*\) and either cut,
\[
 |\mathcal P_I[h]-\mathcal P_I[h_*]|
 \le 2\cdot10^{105}\,(2\|h_*\|_{\mathcal H}+\|e\|_{\mathcal H})\|e\|_{\mathcal H}.
\]
For \(\|e\|_{\mathcal H}<10^{-118}\) the right-hand side is \(<0.041\) even using \(2B\) in place of \(2\|h_*\|\). The audited lower/upper endpoints give positive early and negative packet sign with margin \(>3.31\) and \(>200.05\), respectively. The same argument gives a negative added-window projection \(<-203.36\). The class includes genuine profile changes via nonzero smooth interior variations, and small left/right changes in any bump support strictly inside \((a,d)\) that stay within the ball. One-sided inward width changes of the original flat bump approach it in \(H^4\) by dilation continuity. Outward width changes past the fixed-support interval are outside this theorem.

Uniformly on this ball, \(|\mathcal P_{[a,t]}-\mathcal P_{[a,s]}|\le2\cdot10^{105}B^2|t-s|^{1/2}\) by the leading sup bound and returning energy. Taking \(\Delta=10^{-260}\), the early and packet signs persist at \(d_e+\Delta\) and \(d-\Delta\). This gives a strictly interior common crossing bracket, with a very small quantitative exclusion of the endpoints. The exact integral is continuous and its integrand must be negative on a set of positive measure between the two cuts. There is no uniqueness or pointwise flux sign theorem.

For an exact matched two-amplitude family let \(\mathcal N_{\epsilon,\eta}\) be the tensor news in a Hilbert space with the harmonic normalized to the above ordered scalar amplitudes. Put \(A=\mathcal N_{0,\eta}/\eta\) and \(D=(\mathcal N_{\epsilon,\eta}-\mathcal N_{0,\eta})/(\epsilon^2\eta)\) for nonzero amplitudes. If the matched common-clock estimates \(\|A-T_0h\|\le A_e,\ \|D-T_2h\|\le D_e\) hold on each cut, then the exact energy difference divided by \(3r_0\epsilon^2\eta^2/(4\pi G_4)\) is \(\langle A,D\rangle+\epsilon^2\|D\|^2/2\). It differs from \(\mathcal P_I[h]\) by at most
\[
 A_e\|T_2h\|+(\|T_0h\|+A_e)D_e
 +\tfrac12\epsilon^2(\|T_2h\|+D_e)^2 .
\]
A uniform bound less than \(3.31\) preserves both comparisons on the certified ball. The conditional calculation uses the exact seeded solution at \(\epsilon=0\) as the reference and cancels all seed-only energy terms. Absolute exact tensor energy is positive semidefinite. A uniform estimate for \(D\) requires a common-frame exact constrained family and mixed trace regularity, including control of any additional tensor harmonics. A spherical unseeded evolution has zero tensor news by symmetry. A scalar radion result supplies no uniform estimate for this tensor quotient.
