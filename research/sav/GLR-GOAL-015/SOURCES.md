# Sources inspected

The exact repository, source parent, audit identities and inherited
assumptions are in [INPUTS](INPUTS.md). The physical hierarchy comes
from the GOAL-011 source, GOAL-013 [finite-cut and source records](../GLR-GOAL-013/SOURCE-LEDGER.md),
GOAL-014 [compact and adjoint bounds](../GLR-GOAL-014/REPORT.md),
and the separately pinned independent Work audits. The coefficient
construction specifies the inherited Taylor flow and the newly
checked real-axis and conservation routes in [source](source/ENCLOSURES.md).

For normalization, the author versions inspected were
[Martel–Poisson, v1, Secs. V–VI and Eq. (6.16)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0502028v1)
for the odd master and radiative normalization,
[Mädler–Winicour, v3, Eq. (56)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.01731v3)
for the Bondi angular shift, and
[Flanagan–Nichols, v5, Eq. (2.11a)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.03386v5)
for the mass-aspect tensor flux. The new physical sign bounds are
derived from the pinned ordered equations, rather than inferred from
these general normalization references.
