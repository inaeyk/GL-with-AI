The expansion of Hess_1,vv contains -f_r (Omega W+f W_r)/2.
Its W_r term cancels against the corresponding +f f_r W_r/2 in
f Hess_1,vr. The Omega W term keeps the negative sign. The incorrect
formula used +(f_r/2) Omega W. After the correction all reported
test radii overlap the independent radial mean quadrature.
