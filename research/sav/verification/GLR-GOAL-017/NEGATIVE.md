# Failure modes and proposed corrections

1. **Malformed input can produce false positive gate margins.** A NaN in a late candidate ordinate leaves the initialized subbox minimum at exactly 100 even though earlier computed norms are NaN. A folded time grid can pass the endpoint checks. Require finite aligned arrays, strict monotonicity and the recorded digest for a pinned replay. Check the finiteness of every margin and initialize the reduction from a validated first subbox. The actual reproduced archive satisfies these conditions and its thresholds have independent support. [Reproducer](malformed_archive.py) · [Computation](COMPUTATION.md).
2. **Numerical convergence and warning.** The clustered march retains the DOP853 invalid-divide warning in the exterior IVP, and early coarse marches and alternative readouts vary significantly. A matching digest and mesh differences identify a candidate. Physical error also requires the conforming mixed and scalar residuals, source/contact errors and controlled cap transfer. In the strong estimator an outgoing-ray derivative can be large with small undifferentiated current errors. Piecewise jumps contribute measures unless they cancel or the lift conforms. [Source failure ledger](../../GLR-GOAL-017/checks/FAILURES.md) · [Physical controls](PHYSICAL.md).
3. **Weak witness terminal conditions.** The sine tests used in a scalar witness can have nonzero derivative at the final cut. Applying the integrated mixed adjoint then requires its terminal contact, or a different test with zero derivative there. Carry graph terms, face jumps and exterior norms independently. Earlier GOAL-015 signs and the early cap certificate remain valid on their domains. [Physical assessment](PHYSICAL.md) · [Adversarial assessment](ADVERSARIAL.md).
4. **Residues and cut identification.** A source perturbation delayed past the early cuts can preserve both pole pairs and change later window news in the enlarged scalar class. Interpret the threshold locations $0$, $2\Omega r_0$ and $\Omega r_0\pm ikr_0$ as branch endpoints of the nested resolvents and possible singularities of the completed projection, pending a nonzero projected jump or another exclusion of cancellation. A positive-line cutoff preserves the finite waveform and can change residues. [Spectral analysis](LITERATURE.md).
5. **Different finite-window tests.** The baseline rejection fixes the physical residue and clock. The free phase and amplitude projection has a different comparison and gives a smaller residual. In the return, both template rejections would be consistent with a zero return, which fails each nonzero template by a ratio of one. Report the smaller physical error gate or an independent nonzero witness for ordered signal separation. Finite-seed conclusions additionally need uniform exact trace derivatives.
