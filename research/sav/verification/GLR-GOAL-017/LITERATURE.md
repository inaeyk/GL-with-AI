## Positive-line inversion and sign checks

Write \(w_{tt}-w_{xx}+Vw=-S\), \(V=f(6/r^2-3/r^3)\), \(r_0=1\). Free characteristic integration gives \(N_{\rm vol}=-\frac12\int S(u+x,x)\,dx\). The zero-displacement graph \(t=r(x)-x\) with velocity \(w_t\) gives \(N_\Sigma=+fw_t/2\). To see the latter, take a free field \(P(u)+Q(v)\). Differentiating its zero graph value gives \(P'+[f/(f-2)]Q'=0\) and \(w_t=P'+Q'\), so \(P'=fw_t/2\). The signed transform with \(g_\Sigma=fw_t\) is therefore \(\int e^{-s(u-a)}[\int A S\,dx-A(s,X(u))g_\Sigma(u)]du\) with \(A=-m/(2m_\infty)\). A single constant transfer for the graph would discard the \(X(u)\) dependence. The free control tests the relative sign and yields no missing factor of two. An independent positive-line denominator argument uses \(m''+2sm'=Vm\), \(\Re s=\gamma>0\), and the rescaled real current
\[
Y=|m|^2+\Re(\bar m m'/s),\qquad
Y'=\gamma (|m'|^2+V|m|^2)/|s|^2.
\]
Since \(Y(-\infty)=1\), \(m'(+\infty)=0\) and \(m_\infty\) exists by the integrable Volterra kernel, \(|m_\infty|\ge1\). For the sharper uniform transfer bound put \(a_+=m+m'/(2s)\), \(a_-=-m'/(2s)\). Then \(Y=|a_+|^2-|a_-|^2\), \(a_+'=V(a_++a_-)/(2s)\), and \(|a_-/a_+|<1\). Integrating \(\partial_x\log|a_+|\ge(V/2)(\gamma/|s|^2-1/|s|)\) toward infinity gives \(|A|\le e^{M/(8\gamma)}\) for \(M=\int V\,dx=9/2\). Its application requires a directed physical source error in that mixed norm, and a graph error with its own varying kernel.

\[
R_s[b](x)=\frac{1}{2s}\left(\int_x^\infty V(z)b(z)\,dz+

\]
Both integration ranges partition the radial line, giving \(\|R_s\|\le M/(2|s|)\). The correction to the free multiplier is \(R_s[m]/(2m_\infty)\). ## Independent compact source control

Let \(P(s)=(s-s_q)(s-\bar s_q)(s-s_*)(s-\bar s_*)\). In the scalar comparison class with variable source, take a real \(\chi\in C_c^\infty((0,d))\) and \(\rho\in C_c^\infty((X(0),\infty))\) with \(\int\rho\,dx=1\). Start with \(F_\omega(t,x)=\rho(x)\chi(t-x)\cos(\omega(t-x))\) and add the compact spacetime source \(P(\partial_t)F_\omega\). Its support lies to the future of the graph in that annulus and in \(u>0\), so it preserves the earlier waveform and the graph pair. The time derivatives commute with the stationary operator and its retarded inverse when applied to this source with vanishing graph jets. On the positive line the new news transform equals \(P(s)\) times the news transform of \(F_\omega\). This kills every simple residue at the four specified poles on the continued sheet. There is no need to assume the altered trace has no other poles or cut.

The alteration is nonzero on \(I\) for large \(\omega\). The transfer of \(\rho\) is \(-1/2+O(|s|^{-1})\) uniformly at large frequency on the positive line by the Volterra estimate above. The free term \(-P(\partial_u)[\chi(u)\cos(\omega u)]/2\) has weighted \(L^2(I)\) norm of order \(\omega^4\) by the leading derivative and oscillatory averaging. Its scattering correction has weighted global norm \(O(\omega^3)\) by Plancherel. In that estimate the Fourier transforms of the exponentially weighted bump are Schwartz functions centered at \(\pm\omega\), and the bounded low-frequency multiplier acts only on their rapidly decaying tails. Hence the compact source supplies arbitrarily large window changes by real scaling while keeping the chosen residues and the early waveform. This differs from the source's exterior construction, which prescribes an entire trace transform. It is a counterexample to an inference from residue and early data alone in the variable-source scalar problem, with no alteration allowed in the fixed Einstein experiment.
