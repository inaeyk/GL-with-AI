## Corrections and limitations

- Use $\int N_{01}N_{21}$ for the complete ordered interference and reserve the empirical values near $+7.8181$ and $-1455.615$ for their indicated candidate calculations. The early strong norm bound is tied to the exact affine ordinates and abscissae. The full-packet signed result uses no pilot data. The packet window ends at the nominal launch width and does not integrate the entire scattered waveform. [Source report][report] · [Replay and checks](REPRODUCTION.md).
- State the early weak proof's actual margin and its relation to the source charter. The directed lower optical reference from the frozen run is $(70.1739893-51.3360772)/4\simeq4.70948$, with the other compact bound about $1.28512$ in projection units and the terminal primitive error below $16.66$. The inherited $p_-\ge7.80$ allocation concerned a different candidate strategy. The physical positivity criterion and strong $E_c\le400$ design target are met. [Charter][goal] · [Early proof][adjoint] · [Weak audit](WEAK.md).
- In replay automation, assert the expected positivity or negativity outcome in addition to command success. The first packet calculation exits successfully with a false sign flag. The final signed script also prints its sign test without asserting it. On the pinned grid the Arb comparisons selecting the graph wedge are strictly decided. For altered subdivisions, treat an indeterminate comparison conservatively as a possible wedge, or fail with an explicit disjointness assertion. These are robustness corrections to the scripts. [Failure record](NEGATIVE.md) · [Replay and checks](REPRODUCTION.md).
- Keep the $+k$ half-amplitude, canonical tilted jets, fixed-mass integration constant and the physical endpoint pullback explicit if changing the preparation or comparing to a waveform. A constant coordinate-clock shift on fixed coordinate cuts can add an endpoint term. The matched energy coefficient is invariant under a common pullback. [Geometry](GEOMETRY.md) · [Observables](OBSERVABLES.md).
- The independently reconstructed checks use the pinned GL mode existence and normalization and the GOAL-013/014 physical exterior envelopes. The audit did not re-prove the full spectral problem or rebuild the earlier exterior certificates from first principles. Local exact Ricci tests exercise the source map, and replay exercises the interval implementations. Neither by itself establishes the complete continuum theorem. [Source enclosure][source-geo] · [GOAL-014 audit][review14].
